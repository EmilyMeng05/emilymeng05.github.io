PatternMiner: Automated Mathematical Discovery Through LLM-Guided Training and Interpretation of Small Models

Michael Ruofan Zeng · Junaid Hasan · Xuanyu Yang · Emily Xia Meng · Henry Kvinge

How can machine learning help us discover mathematics, rather than only predict answers? PatternMiner combines an LLM agent with smaller models and their interpretations to propose mathematical conjectures, test them against counterexamples, and refine them. The paper explores this approach across problems in algebraic combinatorics.

Studying the Effects of Task Diversity Using Small Mathematical Worlds

Emily Xia Meng · Xuanyu Yang · Junaid Hasan · Michael Ruofan Zeng · Henry Kvinge

What happens when a model learns several related mathematical tasks instead of just one? We introduce IntWorld and PermWorld, two controlled collections of mathematical tasks that help us study how task variety affects learned representations and generalization. Our initial findings suggest that greater task diversity can enrich a model's internal representations.
