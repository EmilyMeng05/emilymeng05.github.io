Research.

I like research because it lets me follow curiosity seriously. Often, it starts with a question I cannot quite let go of. How do we interpret dreams, for example? I want to understand what we already know, how researchers study such questions, and which methods or comparisons might help us learn more. From there, I can begin asking what I might explore differently.

A question I'm exploring

How do students and AI models learn unfamiliar mathematics?

Teaching has made me curious about how students move from familiar procedures to new mathematical ideas. In the MATH AI Lab, I began thinking about how to compare the difficulties students encounter with those faced by models learning related tasks. I am exploring what such a comparison could reveal about learning and generalization.
