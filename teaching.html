Curiosity / Learning together

Teaching.

I like teaching because understanding something and helping someone else understand it are two different challenges.

Sometimes, a concept that feels familiar to me is completely new to someone else. Teaching reminds me to slow down, question what I take for granted, and look for another way to explain an idea.

The best moments are when a student begins to see why something works, rather than just which formula to use.

How I approach teaching

There is rarely just one way to understand a problem.

I enjoy listening to how students think through a question. When someone gets stuck, I try to find out which part feels unfamiliar, then work from what they already understand. That might mean drawing a picture, walking through a concrete example, or explaining the same idea from a different angle.

A small example

When helping students learn probability, I found that a formula alone was not always enough. Sometimes a diagram, a familiar scenario, or a step-by-step breakdown of the sample space made the idea click. Seeing different approaches work for different students is one of my favorite parts of teaching.
