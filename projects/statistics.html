STAT 451 / Group data visualization project

Threatened Species.

How has the number of threatened species changed over time, and what can we learn from differences across countries and species groups?

The question

Understanding patterns in biodiversity.

Our group explored the United Nations threatened-species dataset because we wanted to better understand the scale and geography of biodiversity threats. We looked at vertebrates, invertebrates, plants, and overall totals across countries and years.

The analysis

Looking beyond one chart.

We built a series of visualizations to examine changes over time, compare countries with high numbers of threatened species, and explore which countries stood out within different species groups. Using line charts and bar charts helped us move between a global view and more detailed geographic comparisons.

We also considered which years showed the most extreme values. These visualizations offered a starting point for asking more specific environmental questions, while reminding us that descriptive trends alone cannot establish their causes.
