PatternMiner.

Automated Mathematical Discovery Through LLM-Guided Training and Interpretation of Small Models

The research question

Can AI help discover mathematical patterns?

PatternMiner studies a workflow in which an LLM agent uses small trained models and information about their behavior to propose mathematical rules, test conjectures, and revise them using evidence and counterexamples.

What interests me

Beyond finding a correct answer

An important question is whether examining a model's internal behavior can provide useful evidence for discovering a rule that generalizes beyond the examples used to develop it. This is part of a broader interest in understanding what a model has learned, rather than only how accurately it predicts.
