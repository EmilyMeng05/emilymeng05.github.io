How does task diversity shape what models learn?

UW MATH AI Lab · March 2026–present

Our work explores how training on related mathematical tasks affects learned representations, transfer and generalization.

First-author publication · 2026

Studying the Effects of Task Diversity Using Small Mathematical Worlds

Emily Xia Meng, Xuanyu Yang, Junaid Hasan, Michael Ruofan Zeng, Henry Kvinge

Accepted for poster presentation at the NeurIPS 2026 MATH-AI Workshop.

arXivOpenReviewResearch code

Two small mathematical worlds

Procedurally generated tasks let us vary training diversity in a setting where answers can be checked precisely.

IntWorld

Integer operations, arithmetic and related tasks.

PermWorld

Properties and statistics of permutations.

What we are learning

Initial experiments suggest that exposure to more related tasks can enrich internal representations. This does not imply that models reliably solve novel tasks without further training. We distinguish predictive accuracy, transfer and the information recoverable from representations.
